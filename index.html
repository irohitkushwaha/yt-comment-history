How to Find Your Comment History Manually

Want to know how to navigate the official menus yourself? Choose your platform below for a complete step-by-step walk-through.

Step-by-Step Desktop Guide

  1. 1

    Access YouTube & Log In

    Go to YouTube.com in your desktop browser. Make sure you are signed in to the Google account associated with your active channel.

  2. 2

    Open Your Library History

    Click on the **three-line menu (☰)** in the top-left corner to open the sidebar. Select **History** from the list of options.

  3. 3

    Navigate to Manage Comments

    On the right side of the History page, you will see a menu block labeled **Manage all history**. Beneath it, click on the option named **Comments**.

  4. 4

    View, Edit, or Delete Comments

    A new tab opens the **Google My Activity YouTube Comments** board. Your comments are presented chronologically. You can click on the video link to edit it, or click the **"X"** to delete a comment instantly.

Step-by-Step Mobile App Guide

  1. 1

    Open Settings in YouTube App

    Launch the YouTube App on your iOS or Android device. Tap the **You** tab (profile picture) in the bottom-right corner, then tap the **Settings (gear icon)** in the top-right corner.

  2. 2

    Select Manage All History

    In the Settings list, tap **Manage all history**. If prompted, select the specific Google account or brand channel you want to examine.

  3. 3

    Navigate to Interactions

    On the resulting My Activity screen, tap the **Interactions** tab located in the top menu list (next to History and Saving Activity).

  4. 4

    Tap Comments & Replies

    Scroll down to the Video Interactions section and tap **Comments & replies**. You will see your chronological history of YouTube comments, where you can read and delete them.
