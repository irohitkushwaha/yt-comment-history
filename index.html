How to Find Your Comment History Manually

Want to know how to navigate the official menus yourself? Choose your platform below for a complete step-by-step walk-through.

Step-by-Step Desktop Guide

  1. 1

    Go to YouTube & Switch to the Correct Channel

    Open your web browser, navigate to YouTube.com, and make sure you are signed in to your Google Account. If your email manages multiple channels or a brand account, click on your profile picture in the top-right corner, click **Switch account**, and select the specific profile whose comment history you want to check.

  2. 2

    Access the History Sidebar Menu

    Look at the top-left corner of the YouTube homepage and click the **three horizontal lines (hamburger menu ☰)** to expand the main navigation sidebar. From the list of options, click on **History**.

  3. 3

    Click the Comments Option in the Manage Panel

    Once on the History page, locate the sidebar panel on the right side of the screen under the **Manage all history** heading. Click on the **Comments** button to proceed. (If your browser window is narrow and the right panel is hidden, scroll to the bottom of the page or expand your browser window to reveal this menu).

  4. 4

    View, Edit, or Delete Your Past Comments

    A new tab will automatically open the official **Google My Activity YouTube Comments** dashboard. Here, your entire comment history is listed in reverse chronological order. To edit or reply to a comment, click the video title or thumbnail link to go to that video. To delete a comment, click the **"X"** button next to it and confirm the deletion; it will be permanently erased.

Step-by-Step Mobile App Guide

  1. 1

    Open Settings in the YouTube App

    Launch the official YouTube app on your Android or iOS device. Tap your profile tab icon (labeled **"You"**) in the bottom-right corner of the screen, and then tap the **Settings gear icon ⚙️** located in the top-right corner to open your account settings.

  2. 2

    Select Manage All History

    In the Settings menu, scroll down and tap **Manage all history**. If you have multiple Google Accounts or brand channels configured on your device, select the exact channel profile you want to inspect when prompted.

  3. 3

    Navigate to the Interactions Tab

    On the Google My Activity screen that loads, locate the horizontal menu tabs at the top of the interface: "History", "Saving activity", and "Interactions". Tap the **Interactions** tab.

  4. 4

    Tap Comments & Replies

    Scroll down the Interactions page until you find the **Video interactions** section. Under this section, locate and tap on **Comments & replies**.

  5. 5

    View and Manage Your Comments

    The app will display your chronological YouTube comments log. You can review your past comments, tap the video links to open the original videos, or tap the **"X"** icon next to any comment to delete it permanently from Google's records.
